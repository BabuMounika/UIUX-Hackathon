### Overview of the FreshTrack Home Screen (Landing Page)
The displayed screen is the Home / Landing Page of the FreshTrack application — a food-tracking and waste-reduction platform.
It is designed to introduce the app’s purpose, highlight key features, and guide users toward beginning their food-inventory journey.

### Description of the Screen
## 1. Header Navigation Bar
At the top of the screen, a clean and minimal navigation bar provides quick access to:
•	Inventory – View and manage food items stored at home
•	Recipes – Find recipes using ingredients about to expire
•	Stats – View food-waste analytics
•	Tips – Get useful kitchen and food-storage tips
A green “Add Food” button on the right allows users to quickly add new pantry items.

### 2. Hero Section (Main Banner)
This section features:
•	A large, vibrant image of fresh vegetables to represent the app’s focus on food freshness and sustainability.
•	A bold headline:
“Reduce Food Waste, Save Money”
The message clearly communicates the value proposition in a simple, impactful way.

### 3. Supporting Message
Below the hero image, there is a short description:
“Track your pantry, get expiry alerts, and discover recipes that use ingredients before they spoil.”
### 4. Primary Call-to-Action Buttons
Two buttons guide users into the app:
•	Start Tracking Food — primary action, encouraging immediate engagement
•	Learn How It Works — secondary action for first-time users seeking more information
Both buttons support user onboarding and help users explore the app.

### 5. Feature Highlight Section
At the bottom, three core features of the app are showcased with icons:

✔ Reduce Waste

Helps users lower their household food waste through smart tracking.

✔ Expiry Alerts

Sends timely reminders before ingredients expire.

✔ Smart Recipes

Suggests recipes based on items that are nearing expiry.

This section quickly communicates what makes the app useful and unique.

### Summary
This tab/screen functions as the user’s first interaction point with the FreshTrack app.
Its goals are to:
•	Introduce the purpose of the application
•	Show users how the app helps them save food and money
•	Provide intuitive navigation to core features
•	Encourage users to start using the app immediately

### Overview of the “Your Food Inventory” Tab – FreshTrack App
This screen represents the Food Inventory Management tab of the FreshTrack application.
It provides users with a clean and organized interface to monitor their stored food items, track expiry dates, and take action to prevent waste. The primary goal of this tab is to help users stay aware of the freshness of their food and make timely decisions based on expiry alerts.

### Detailed Description of the Screen
### 1. Top Navigation Bar
The header includes key app sections:
•	Inventory – current screen
•	Recipes – find meals using items that are expiring
•	Impact – view personal food waste analytics
•	Tips – helpful guidance for storage and food safety
On the right, a notification icon with a badge alerts users about items needing attention, and a green “+ Add Food” button enables quick item addition.

### 2. Page Header: “Your Food Inventory”
This section clearly establishes that users are viewing their stored food list.
A short description—“Track your items and their expiry dates”—reinforces the purpose of this screen.
A secondary green button “+ Add Food Item” sits below, offering another entry point for adding items.

### 3. Food Item Cards (Core Content Area)
Each food item is displayed inside an individual card, making the list visually organized and easy to scan.
Each card includes:
## a. Food Name & Status Label
•	Organic Spinach – Expiring (orange)
•	Greek Yogurt – Fresh (green)
•	Sourdough Bread – Expired (red)
•	Bell Peppers – Fresh
•	Chicken Breast – Expiring
These color-coded labels instantly communicate the condition of each item.
## b. Category
Examples: Vegetables, Dairy, Bakery, Meat
This helps users mentally group items.
## c. Quantity
Displayed clearly in bold (e.g., 1 bag, 500g, 3 pieces).
## d. Expiry Date
Shown with a calendar icon to make scanning easier.
## e. Freshness Indicator Bar
A colored status bar under each item shows urgency:
•	Green – Fresh
•	Orange – Expiring soon
•	Red – Already expired
This provides at-a-glance awareness without needing to read dates.
## f. "Find Recipes" Button
Appears for expiring items.
This drives user action—helping them cook items before they spoil.
 Summary of User Experience
This tab is designed to:
•	Give users real-time insights into the state of their food items
•	Promote quick decision-making through color-coded indicators
•	Reduce food waste by providing expiry awareness
•	Offer smart actions such as recipe suggestions
•	Make navigation simple and intuitive

### Overview of the “Recipes” Tab – FreshTrack App

The Recipes tab of the FreshTrack application is designed to help users reduce food waste by generating recipe suggestions based on items in their pantry that are expiring soon. This tab empowers users to take immediate action by transforming near-expiry ingredients into meals, saving money and reducing food waste.
The screen offers a simple, inviting, and intuitive layout with a focus on clarity and actionability.

## Detailed Description of the Screen
## 1. Header Section
At the top, the FreshTrack branding is displayed along with a tagline:
“Save food, save money, save the planet 🌍”
This reinforces the app’s sustainability mission.
## 2. Navigation Menu
A horizontal navigation bar allows users to switch between key sections:
•	My Pantry
•	Expiring Soon
•	Recipes (current tab – highlighted)
•	Impact
•	Tips
The Recipes tab is visually emphasized with a green outline, indicating the active section.
## 3. Suggested Recipes Section
The main content area is titled:
## 🍽 Suggested Recipes
“Based on your expiring ingredients”
This text clearly explains the purpose of the section and sets user expectations.
## 4. Recipe Cards
Each recipe is shown within a soft-colored, rounded card for easy readability and visual grouping.
Every recipe card contains:
### a. Save-Waste Indicator
A small label such as:
•	“Saves 3 items”
•	“Saves 2 items”
•	“Saves 1 item”
This feature highlights the environmental impact and encourages users to cook using the ingredients that would otherwise go to waste.
### b. Recipe Title
Examples shown:
•	Creamy Chicken Pasta
•	Carrot Ginger Soup
•	Greek Yogurt Bowl
Titles are presented boldly for easy scanning.
### c. Ingredients Used
Displayed with small icons, e.g.:
•	Uses: Milk, Chicken
•	Uses: Carrots, Chicken Broth
•	Uses: Yogurt, Fruits
This helps users quickly identify which expiring ingredients will be utilized.
### d. Preparation Time
A time icon indicates how long the recipe takes (e.g., 25 min, 30 min, 5 min).
### e. Action Button – “View Recipe”
A clear green button encourages users to proceed and follow full recipe instructions.
 ## Summary of User Experience & Purpose
 
This tab is designed to:

✔ Provide users with smart, personalized recipe suggestions based on pantry data

✔ Encourage food-saving behaviors by showing how many items each recipe saves

✔ Inspire quick decision-making through simple visuals and cook time indicators

✔ Help reduce food waste by motivating users to cook items nearing expiry

✔ Create a seamless, motivational experience with a clean and modern UI

This screen provides monthly waste-reduction analytics, showcasing the user’s contribution in terms of:
•	Food saved
•	Money saved
•	CO₂ emissions prevented
The purpose is to motivate users, reinforce sustainable behavior, and create a sense of achievement through clear and meaningful impact metrics.
##  Detailed Description of the Screen
### 1. Header & Navigation
The top section displays:
•	FreshTrack logo and sustainability tagline — “Save food, save money, save the planet 🌍”
•	A soft, pastel navigation bar with five tabs:
My Pantry | Expiring Soon | Recipes | Impact | Tips
The Impact tab is highlighted, indicating the current view.
### 2. Section Title – “Your Impact”
This title introduces the purpose of the page:
Your Impact
Track your waste reduction progress
It sets the tone for a motivating, encouraging experience.
### 3. Impact Metric Cards
Three large, visually appealing cards summarize the user’s achievements for the month:
## a. Food Saved
•	12.5 kg saved
•	Displayed with a leaf icon
•	Green color theme indicating environmental benefit
This quantifies how much food the user prevented from being wasted.
## b. Money Saved
•	$87 saved this month
•	Represented with a money-bag icon
•	Uses a warm peach background to convey financial positivity
This shows the economic impact of mindful food usage.
## c. CO₂ Prevented
•	31.2 kg of CO₂ prevented
•	Earth/globe icon signifies environmental sustainability
•	Green typography emphasizes positive environmental action
This metric highlights how reducing food waste contributes to lowering carbon footprint.
## 4. Monthly Goal Progress
A dedicated progress area shows the user’s advancement toward their Waste Reduction Goal.
•	A long progress bar visualizes completion — 78% achieved
•	A motivational message:
“You’re doing great! Keep it up 🎉”
This section reinforces positive behavior and keeps users motivated to continue reducing waste.
 ## Summary of User Experience & Purpose
The Impact tab provides users with:

✔ Motivational feedback on their sustainability progress

✔ Clear and meaningful metrics that relate to real-world benefits

✔ A visually clean and uplifting layout

✔ A monthly progress bar to encourage goal completion

✔ Positive reinforcement through friendly messages

Overall, this tab transforms food-saving efforts into tangible achievements, helping users stay engaged and aware of the environmental and financial benefits of using FreshTrack.

At the top, the FreshTrack brand appears along with its sustainability tagline:
“Save food, save money, save the planet 🌍”
Below it is a navigation bar with the five main sections:
•	My Pantry
•	Expiring Soon
•	Recipes
•	Impact
•	Tips (highlighted as active)
### 2. Section Title – “Food Waste Tips”
Large and bold, the title clearly introduces the purpose of the tab:
Food Waste Tips
Learn how to keep food fresh longer
An orange icon of a lightbulb reinforces the idea of helpful insights and smart hacks.
### 3. Tips Display Cards
The content is organized in neatly arranged, rectangular cards with rounded edges.
Each card contains:
